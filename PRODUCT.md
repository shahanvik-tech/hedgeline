# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Treasury and risk teams at midsize companies in logistics, agriculture, hospitality, airlines, insurance, and retail. They assess operational event losses and manage ongoing hedging programs.

## Product Purpose

Hedgeline maps business exposures to event contracts, sizes paper hedges, tracks coverage and gaps, and creates decision memos for finance leadership.

## Positioning

A corporate event-risk hedging desk, organized around a risk register and recurring program rather than one-off market speculation.

## Operating Context

Users record a loss event, date window, loss amount, and likelihood; evaluate public Kalshi and Polymarket contracts; assess settlement and basis risk; book paper positions; monitor premium, coverage, settlement dates, and indicative mark to market; and print a CFO or board memo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, SQLite. Public market APIs are proxied server-side and cached for five minutes. Bundled reference contracts are visibly labeled snapshot data. No order placement or real trading. One contract pays $1 on Yes resolution. Event hedges usually do not qualify for US GAAP hedge accounting.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Hedgeline. Institutional finance tone, plain precise copy, off-white #FAFAF7 ground, near-black text, one deep green or ink-blue accent, neutral grays. Inter or IBM Plex Sans for UI, IBM Plex Mono for figures. Thin 1px borders, tight hierarchy, radius at most 6px. No purple gradients, glassmorphism, emoji, glow, or generic card UI.

## Evidence on Hand

Three synthetic demo companies and six synthetic exposures live in the local database seed. Public market feeds and reference snapshots supply indicative data. No testimonials or commercial performance claims are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Keep the exposure and business loss central to every decision.
- Distinguish market resolution from business outcome.
- Label paper and snapshot data clearly.
- Make coverage gaps and accounting timing visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive layout and keyboard accessible controls are required.
